Contact

Found a bug, a wrong result, or want a tool added? Tell us and include the input that caused the problem when you can. Messages go to the person who runs the site, and we aim to reply within a few working days.

Email: contact@streamerosai.com

What helps most

  • The page address and what you expected to see.
  • The input that caused a wrong result, with anything private removed, and the browser you used.
  • For a mistake in a guide, the sentence and the source you believe says otherwise.

What we cannot do

We cannot give personal financial, tax, legal or employment advice, check a specific document, or recover a file for you, because nothing you enter is uploaded to us. The site is run by one person, so replies can take a few days. Reports of wrong results are fixed first. If you do not want us to keep your email address, say so and we will delete the message once it is answered.
